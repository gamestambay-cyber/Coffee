OUR STORY

Roasted in small batches,
brewed with intention.

Maples Bean began in August 2025 with a simple goal: to provide quality food and beverages that bring people together. Founded by Mr. Jordan Romero, Maples Bean was created to be more than just a coffee shop—it is a welcoming space where the community can enjoy delicious food, refreshing beverages, and a warm, comfortable atmosphere. From the very beginning, we have been committed to serving our customers with quality, care, and genuine hospitality, one cup and one meal at a time.
